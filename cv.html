Hullo,

I'm Rob, a designer with almost 30 years of experience building digital products and leading high-performing design teams.

I started my career in the early days of the web, crafting HTML and CSS, and building flaky backends with PHP. Since then, I've led design at places like Hailo, Trainline, and Hedgehog, and love getting deep into the craft and exploring the new tools that are quickly changing how we design.

When not at work, I'm tinkering with side projects, such as Albatross, a Novel Writing app for the Mac, and Look Up, an ISS Tracker. I also love to write and spend much of my free time writing sci-fi.

Experience

Head of Design @ Hedgehog → Inveniam

Reporting to CTO

Dec 2021 – Present

I joined Hedgehog, where our goal was to build the end-to-end digital infrastructure to power the distribution of real-world assets to individual investors. In 2025, we were acquired by Inveniam, and our focus shifted towards building the tools allowing institutions to turn unstructured data into verified, trusted, structured data that's AI-agent ready.

Head of Design @ Trainline

Reporting to CPO

Feb 2016 – Jan 2020 · 4 yrs

Trainline is the world's leading independent digital rail platform, selling tickets on behalf of over 270 rail and coach companies across 45 countries.

I joined Trainline to build a world-class design team to work cross-functionally with product and engineering to deliver on our mission to provide smarter journeys to our customers. Reporting to the CPO and working with product and engineering leadership, I grew the team from three to 20+ and led design through a rebrand, a re-platforming, European expansion, and eventually an IPO.

Trainline today is the most downloaded travel app in the UK and regularly tops the charts in both the "Travel" and "Transportation" categories on iOS and Android.
